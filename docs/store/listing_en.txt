Solve the puzzle in fewer moves to earn up to three stars. Every finished game counts toward your personal stats.

Hand-drawn jungle art, wooden tiles with satisfying animations, calm background music, sounds and vibration (all switchable in Settings).

Leave anytime — your unfinished puzzle is waiting for you when you come back.

No ads. No in-app purchases. Just a great puzzle.
