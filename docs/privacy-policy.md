# Privacy Policy — Puzzle 15

_Last updated: October 9, 2026_

Puzzle 15 ("the app") is a sliding-puzzle game made by VISION SYSTEMS CORP ("we"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, and how you can delete it.

## What we collect

| Data | When | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Account ID** (a random Firebase user ID) | When you sign in with Google or play as a guest | To keep your world records under your account |
| **Google account email and name** (held by Firebase Authentication) | Only if you sign in with Google | To let you sign in again on another phone |
| **Player name** you choose (3–16 characters) | When you save your name | Shown next to your results on the world leaderboard |
| **Best results** (moves and time for 3×3, 4×4 and 5×5) | When you finish a game with a new personal best | To rank you on the world leaderboard |
| **App usage and diagnostics** (Firebase Analytics: app opens, screens, device model, OS version, an app instance ID) | While you use the app | To understand how the app is used and improve it |

Your **player name and best results are public**: anyone using the app can see them on the world leaderboard. Do not use your real full name if you do not want it shown.

Stored **only on your phone** and never uploaded: unfinished games, your full game history and statistics, and your settings (music, sound, vibration, board size).

We do **not** collect your location, contacts, photos or payment details, and the app shows **no ads**.

## How it is processed

The data above is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Analytics), operated by Google LLC under the [Firebase privacy terms](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy). Cloud data is stored in the European Union (Frankfurt). All traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

We do not sell your data or share it with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

## How long we keep it

Your account, name and results are kept until you delete your account. Analytics data is kept by Firebase for up to 14 months.

## Your choices

- **Play as a guest** to avoid linking a Google account.
- **Sign out** in Settings: this also clears the progress stored on the phone.
- **Delete your account** in Settings → _Delete account_. This removes your account, your player name and all your world records right away, and resets the app on the phone. See [Delete your account](account-deletion.md) for other ways to ask for deletion.

## Children

The game is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
