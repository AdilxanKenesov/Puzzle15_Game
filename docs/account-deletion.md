# Delete your Puzzle 15 account

You can delete your account and all data linked to it at any time.

## In the app (recommended)

1. Open **Puzzle 15** and tap the **Settings** (gear) button.
2. Tap **Delete account** under the Sign in / Sign out button.
3. Confirm with **Delete**. If you signed in with Google, you may be asked to choose your Google account again.

The deletion happens immediately.

## Without the app

Email **adilxankenesov11@gmail.com** from the address of your Google account (or include your player name if you played as a guest) with the subject "Delete my Puzzle 15 account". We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

## What is deleted

- Your Firebase account (Google sign-in link or guest ID)
- Your player name
- All your world leaderboard results

Progress stored only on your phone (unfinished games, statistics, settings) is reset by the in-app deletion, or removed when you uninstall the app. Anonymous analytics already collected cannot be linked back to you and expire after at most 14 months.
